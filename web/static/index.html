From enrollment to attendance

  1. Student Details: enter the student’s ID and details. Confirm photo permission, then capture or upload three JPEG/PNG photos showing one face each. Save the student.
  2. Train Data: train the model once your students are enrolled. Adding or removing students requires training again.
  3. Face Detector: enable your camera and start live recognition, capture a single frame, or upload a fresh photo. Scanning pauses when a possible match is found.
  4. Attendance: verify the person’s identity and confirm the match. Review the register and export a CSV before leaving.
My camera is unavailable

Allow camera access for this site in your browser and use HTTPS or localhost. Another application may be using the camera. Uploading photos also works.

No face or no close match

Use a front-facing photo with one unobstructed face and good lighting. Add missing students and retrain the model. LBPH distance measures similarity; it is not an accuracy percentage.

Where are my records?

Records and face samples belong to this browser session. They expire after inactivity and are lost on server restart. Export attendance regularly. The desktop MySQL database is separate.

VISION ATTEND

A complete attendance workflow.

This browser workspace brings the desktop project’s modules together: student management, face capture, model training, face recognition, photo gallery, and attendance records.

Python / FastAPIOpenCV / LBPHHaar face detection

The original desktop application uses Tkinter and MySQL. This browser version uses isolated, temporary sessions and a model trained from your own enrolled samples.

Recognition suggests a possible identity for human review. This prototype has no liveness detection or measured real-world accuracy and is not validated for official attendance decisions.

Your session & data

Photos are sent to this app’s server for face detection and recognition. Face samples, learned descriptors, student details, and attendance are held in memory for this browser session.

  • Up to 10 students, with 3 face samples each.
  • Data is removed within 31 minutes of inactivity and on server restart.
  • No permanent storage or synchronization with the desktop MySQL database.
